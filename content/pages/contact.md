## Freelance work

I take on a small number of freelance projects alongside my job. Things I can help with:

- **LLM features that need to be trustworthy:** question answering over your documents with real citations, assistants that call your tools, and evaluations that tell you whether it works. See [Anuchhed](/projects/anuchhed/) and the [Agentic Eval Framework](/projects/agentic-eval-framework/).
- **Machine-learning models from data you already have:** ranking, classification and forecasting, validated properly so the numbers hold up. See [Trade Filter ML](/projects/trade-filter-ml/) and [Clinic Agent](/projects/clinic-agent/).
- **Small tools that remove manual work:** desktop and web apps for a specific workflow, delivered so that non-technical people can use them. See [ITR Fetch](/projects/itr-fetch/) and the [Invoice Generator](/projects/invoice-generator/).

## How a freelance project works

1. You email me what you need and why. We have a short call if it helps.
2. I write a one-page plan: what I'll build, what's out of scope, a fixed price and a timeline.
3. I build it in small steps you can try along the way.
4. You get the working software, its source code and plain-language documentation.
