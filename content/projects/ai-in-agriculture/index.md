## What it is

A draft review paper, written in LaTeX, on the socioeconomic impacts of artificial intelligence in Indian agriculture, drawing on published research and reports such as the World Economic Forum's AI for Agriculture Innovation initiative.

## Structure

- **Socioeconomic impacts:** productivity and resource efficiency (precision agriculture), rural employment and labour, and food security and livelihoods.
- **Challenges and ethics:** equitable access to AI tools for small farmers, privacy, data ownership and security, and environmental and cultural implications.

## Status

A working draft; not submitted for publication.
