## What it is

My study notes, written to be read again later.

- ***Mathematics for Deep Learning*** (Quarto book): three chapters covering linear algebra and eigendecomposition; single- and multi-variable calculus, gradients and higher-order derivatives; and probability and information theory (entropy and divergences), each with derivations and Python and PyTorch examples.
- **LLM notes** (Typst): how transformers work, and prompt engineering.
- **Basics:** machine-learning fundamentals and NLP topics such as context-free grammars and dependency parsing.
