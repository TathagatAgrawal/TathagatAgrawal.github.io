## Overview

Grokking is a training dynamic in which a network reaches perfect accuracy on its training data, then, only after training for much longer, suddenly generalises to data it has not seen. This paper is a review: it collects when grokking has been observed, the explanations researchers have proposed for it, and the questions that are still open.

It is a survey rather than an experimental paper, so it has no results of its own. The experiments that came out of the same research are in the companion paper, [Analysis of Grokking-Induced Rigidity in Neural Networks using Transfer Learning](/research/grokking-rigidity/).
